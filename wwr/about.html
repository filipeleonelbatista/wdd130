Gravataí White Water Rafting

At Gravataí White Water Rafting, our purpose is to deliver safe, thrilling and all-inclusive river experiences. With certified guides, quality equipment and a commitment to safety and value, we ensure every trip is unforgettable for families and adventurers alike.

History

Founded in 2010 with a single raft on the Gravataí River, our company began as a family passion for the outdoors.

Today we operate daily trips ranging from gentle family floats to challenging Grade 4 rapids, serving over 5,000 guests each season while preserving the river through responsible tourism practices.
